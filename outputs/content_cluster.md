Topical Authority Strategy

1. Cover the main topic deeply

The pillar establishes the central topic around best café in Thiruvalla, while supporting articles cover coffee, food, desserts, different customer groups, and café-selection considerations.

2. Target multiple search intents

The cluster intentionally combines:

Informational: coffee, snacks, desserts, café selection
Commercial investigation: comparing cafés and deciding where to visit
Local: cafés and places to eat in Thiruvalla
Transactional: users searching for a café near me and potentially preparing to visit
